About Me

Bridging tax planning, governance, and intergenerational success.

I help founders, leaders, and family businesses create, preserve, and seamlessly transfer their legacy. As a Chartered Accountant with deep expertise in succession consultancy, corporate structuring, tax strategy, management consulting, and risk analysis, I specialize in navigating complex financial landscapes—particularly with LLPs—to optimize business outcomes and leadership continuity.

Holding certifications in Arbitration, FEMA, AI & ML, FAFD, BRSR, GST, Public Finance & Government Accounting, and Concurrent Audits from ICAI, I bring a 360° governance and strategy lens to every engagement. My approach blends tax planning, governance, change management, and corporate structuring into a single roadmap that inspires investor confidence.

Beyond technical expertise, I am passionate about mentoring and coaching, guiding organizations through leadership transitions while preserving their core culture and vision.
